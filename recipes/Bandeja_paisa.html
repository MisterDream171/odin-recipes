Bandeja Paisa

Bandeja Paisa is a famous Colombian dish that comes from the department of Antioquia. Its unique style makes tourist go crazy with the variety of food that it contains.

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Rice
  • Avocado
  • Sausage
  • Plantain
  • Two potatoes
  • Beans
  • chicharron
  • Arepa

Steps

  1. Prepare the beans, hogao and powdered beef one day ahead and keep in the refrigerator.
  2. Heat the beans and powdered beef and hogao.
  3. Cook the white rice and plantains.
  4. Fry the eggs and chorizos.
  5. Make the chicharrones on a pan.
  6. Place the rice in a tray or platter and place the rest of the ingredients as you like.
  7. Serve the food
