Lasagna

Lasagna is a famous italian dish that many people around the world prepare it in some simple steps.

Ingredients

  • Cheese Filling
  • Meat
  • Sauce
  • Spinach
  • Pasta

Steps

  1. In a large pot of salted water, boil lasagna noodles.
  2. Cook sausage and beef with onion and garlic, this will create meat sauce. After that, drain well, add the pasta sauce and simmer it for a few minutes to thicken.
  3. Stir the cheese mixture together in a bowl.
  4. Layer the meat sauce and cheese mixture with lasagna noodles and bake until the top of the lasagna is golden brown.
  5. Later, spread about a cup of meat sauce into a 9x13 pan. Add a layer of noodles.
  6. Top the noodles with some of the ricotta cheese mixture. Repeat the layers, ending with a layer of noodles and sauce.
  7. Cover with foil and bake. Remove foil, top with mozzarella and parmesan, and bake for another 15 minutes.
  8. Once you have prepared the meat sauce and cheese mixture, you're ready to layer. THis is the order of layers: Sauce-noodles-cheese
